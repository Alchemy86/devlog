AgentGB

Teaching a small local network to play Pokémon Blue from the screen alone — a picture in, a button out, at effectively zero inference cost. One network of 118,806 parameters, given nothing but the 160×144 image, powers on a retail cartridge and works its way from the bedroom out onto Route 1. This is the log of how far it gets, and the single missing skill that stops it going further.

The nine-link chain is one cold boot to Viridian City, nine milestones, seed 0, screen only, no state loads. That 177/300 was measured on ball3b-s0 under argmax, not sampling — and under argmax the entry decision reads right at probability 1.0000, so all 300 boots take the same starter. What it is

AgentGB is a small convolutional network — three strided convolutions and a dense layer, 118,806 parameters in total — that plays from perception alone. It sees four consecutive frames of the screen, reduced to a 40×36 grid of four ink levels, and returns one of six buttons. No memory read of any kind reaches it; it is looking at the same pixels a person would.

It is trained by imitation, not reinforcement — there is no reward and no exploration. A privileged scripted teacher, which is allowed to read memory and run shortest-path search, plays the game first; its play is recorded as a corpus, and a separate training run turns that corpus into the student. The student is what ships; the teacher is scaffolding and is not evidence about the model. Splitting where to go (the teacher's search) from what am I looking at (the network's perception) is why the player is 118,806 parameters rather than millions.

The number we can stand behind

Re-runnable only

The 300/300 nine-link headline this project used to lead with was measured on fix9s0 — a student that always took Charmander, was never committed, and no longer exists on any disk. It cannot be re-run, so it is not a number we quote. The best committed figure is 177 of 300 cold boots (59%), and it belongs to ball3b-s0. Being straight about the rest: the student shipping today is several training generations past ball3b-s0, and no later arm has been given its own N=300 chain run — so 177/300 is the last honest chain measurement, not a current one. We would rather publish a stale number and say it is stale than a flattering one we cannot reproduce.

One missing skill: the Route 1 whiteout

The 59% is not scattered failure — it is a single failure, repeated. The starter decides it. Three arms were trained, one seed each, and run over 300 cold boots apiece: the Squirtle arm completed the chain 300 of 300, the Charmander control 300 of 300, and the Bulbasaur arm 177 of 300 — and every one of those 123 failures ended in a battle on Route 1. These are separate trained networks, not one network with its starter forced; only the varied arm was ever committed, on a deliberate call to ship one model rather than a per-starter zoo.

The cause is mechanical and honest: the rival takes the counter-starter, the student has no healing behaviour yet, and Route 1's wild grass whites out a lead that walked in already hurt. Squirtle survives the same gauntlet on stat advantage alone. So the opening chain is not gated by perception or navigation — it is gated by one skill the student has never been taught. That is the next link, and the scoreboard names exactly how much it is worth.

Skills that cannot damage each other

New behaviours are added without retraining the base, through a mechanism called an AdapterHead: a small, completely separate module that outputs a nudge added to the frozen base policy's own pre-softmax logits before a button is chosen. Its final layer starts at exactly zero, so an untrained adapter contributes nothing, and it is only consulted when its own region recogniser fires. The freezing is proven, not asserted — the trainer takes a SHA-256 of every base layer before and after and refuses to write if any of them changed. New skills therefore cannot quietly damage old ones, because the old ones are literally unwritable while the new one is learned.

The adapter path is an incremental-skill add-on, distinct from the imitation run that trains the main chain student. It is early — two skills so far — and it can only nudge behaviour the base can already see.

Milestones

24 Aug 2026

House default set to sampling, plus a regression net

The driver's default moved to temperature 1.0 with a 0.80 gate, and a fast basics regression suite was added after three separate regressions landed in a single afternoon.

23 Aug 2026

Switch-trap fix becomes the committed student

The shipped weights became pixel-fix7-round1. A masking-only student shipped the same day and was rolled straight back after a filmed regression — 5 of 9 milestones against the prior 8 of 9.

21 Aug 2026

Natural starter variety

A seeded choice in the teacher broke the always-one-starter collapse. Measured on ball3b-s0, sampled at temperature 1 — Bulbasaur 467, Charmander 298, Squirtle 231 of 1,000 — with 996 of 1,000 still reaching the goal.

19 Aug 2026

Out of Pallet Town, into Viridian City

The chain reached nine links end to end, from a cold cartridge to Viridian City — up from 7 of 300 before one missing teacher rule was restored.

17 Aug 2026

The first fight

Six links, validated on three foreign emulators the student had never trained against: 300 of 300 reached the rival battle, 245 of 300 won it.

16 Aug 2026

The screen student appears

Five links, on the screen alone — no memory reads — completing 300 of 300 cold boots. The first version that played the way a person sees the game.
