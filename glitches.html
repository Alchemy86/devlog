The hooked Dragonite

An NPC's pathing routine has two loops with no bound between them. Stand on one specific unreachable tile and the search loop walks off the end of a four-entry table, wraps the whole 64K address space, and the copy loop starts reading 898 bytes of ROM graphics data as a movement script — landing on a wild battle against a level-0 Dragonite with no scripted trigger behind it. Seventeen years documented by others; this is our own reproduction, byte for byte, on TerminalGB's core.

Measured 7 Sep 2026

The trainer who saw him black out

A trainer who spots the player on the frame they faint advances the map's own script before the blackout warp fires. The script index survives the warp and the heal, and hands the player a trainer battle the moment they walk back onto the tile it happened on — the community's trainer escape glitch, caught here in a run nobody set up for it.

Where these come from. Each glitch here has been reproduced on this project's own tooling, not only read about — a step-by-step ROM trace for the hooked Dragonite, a replayed cold boot for the trainer escape. Both are cross-checked against AtlasGB's Gen 1 memory atlas, and where the atlas itself turned out to be incomplete, that is said plainly rather than smoothed over.
