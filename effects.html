Where these come from. Every preview is rendered by the Pokewalker's own pw-shorts fx-gallery command, from the same code the shorts and the walker use. The ones marked Runs on the walker are the integer versions the device itself draws, shown at three times its pixel size.

The creatures. Every creature here is a maths creature: shapes, a skeleton and motion, drawn by our own renderer. No game art is used.
