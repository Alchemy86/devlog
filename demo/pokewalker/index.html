How to use it

Double tap the closed ball to open it. Swipe sideways for the games, up or down to change who walks with you. The crossed swords on the games ring are BATTLE: QUICK BATTLE against a rival matched to your team, or GYM CHALLENGE against the sixteen Gold Gym Leaders in order, each beaten one a badge for the trainer card. Tap the egg at the top for pet mode: drag to look round, double tap to zoom, stroke the Pokémon, flick the ball, tap the radio, the PC or the console. PWR goes back; BOOT is the other key. Walk pretends you are walking, for steps and Watts.

Sound starts with your first tap or key press (browsers keep pages quiet until then). The walker saves itself in this browser as the device saves to its flash: the team, Watts, steps, the bag, pet mode, the Radio's stars and the settings all come back next time.

Every walker is a trainer, as in the games: an ID No. drawn at its first start, a name (set it above, as the phone setup page does on the device), and the walker's name and ID on every Pokémon it catches. The settings list's TRAINER row shows the trainer card.

Two things are hardware and are not here: WiFi (the walker's own hotspot and the phone setup page need its radio) and the link cable to a Game Boy, which the Trade Room needs to actually trade. Their screens open, and say there is nothing connected.

The creatures here are the demo's own. Load sprite folder takes any folder in this layout, read in your browser and never uploaded:

pokemon/0001-NAME/front-2.png    second frame (optional)
pokemon/0001-NAME/walk.png       8 walking frames in a row:
                                 back, front, left, right ×2
pokemon/0001-NAME/walk-shiny.png shiny colours (optional)
room/room.bin                    pet mode's room (optional)
pokemon/0001-NAME/cry.wav        the cry, 8-bit mono WAV (optional)
