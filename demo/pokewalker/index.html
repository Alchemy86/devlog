How to use it

Double tap the closed ball to open it. Swipe sideways for the games, up or down to change who walks with you. Tap the egg at the top for pet mode: drag to look round, double tap to zoom, stroke the Pokémon, flick the ball, tap the radio, the PC or the console. PWR goes back; BOOT is the other key. Walk pretends you are walking, for steps and Watts.

The creatures here are the demo's own. Load sprite folder takes any folder in this layout, read in your browser and never uploaded:

pokemon/0001-NAME/front.png      battle picture (≤96×96)
pokemon/0001-NAME/front-2.png    second frame (optional)
pokemon/0001-NAME/walk.png       8 walking frames in a row:
                                 back, front, left, right ×2
pokemon/0001-NAME/walk-shiny.png shiny colours (optional)
room/room.bin                    pet mode's room (optional)
team.txt                         dex numbers to walk with
