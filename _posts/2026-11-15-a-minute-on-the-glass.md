## What's in it

- The ball shakes, opens on Pikachu and rolls through the team to Charizard, then drops
  into pet mode in Gold's bedroom.
- Surfing Pikachu, the wild areas, and moves played on the walking face with their own
  animations.
- The sixteen gym leaders, Kanto then Johto.
- Stadium battles: Surf, Earthquake and Thunder, a beam clash in the middle of the field,
  and a knockout.
- The shared park, live, and a RALLY race.

The score under it is chip and synth, written for the film.

## How it was made

The ball is the printed shell, rendered in Blender, turning on a reflective floor. Its
screen is not a mock-up. Every scene on the glass was recorded from the walker's Rust
software at its native 412×412, so what plays on the ball is what the walker draws.

The battle that ends the film runs a little longer on its own: two ordinary hits, the clash,
and the knockout.
